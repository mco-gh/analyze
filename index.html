        About Analyze

        Analyze takes a chess game and goes through it with Stockfish, the free, open-source chess engine, running right in your browser. There’s no account and nothing is uploaded.

        Loading a game

        • Paste a game in PGN, the format every chess site exports (on Lichess or Chess.com, look for Share or Export). Comments and side lines are skipped.
        • Or paste just the moves, like 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3, or e2e4 e7e5.
        • Or a FEN, to study a single position, optionally followed by moves.
        • Or a Lichess game link: Analyze fetches the game from Lichess.
        • Or nothing at all: Explorer mode starts from the opening position, and you make the moves for both sides, handy for studying openings. Each move is graded as you play it. Step back and play something else to start a new line from there.
        • Links work too: ?pgn=, ?moves= and ?fen= in the address load a game, ?ply=12 opens it at a move, and ?orient=black turns the board.

        Reading the review

        • The bar beside the board and the graph show who is winning: the share of white is white’s chance of winning, from the engine’s evaluation. Numbers are in pawns (+1.0 is about a pawn up for white); #3 means mate in three.
        • Every move is graded by how much of its player’s winning chances it gave away: ?! inaccuracy, ? mistake or ?? blunder. ★ Best is the engine’s own choice, and ! Great is a best move that was the only good one.
        • Accuracy is out of 100, worked out from every move a player made, the way Lichess does it.
        • The engine panel shows its top three moves where you are, thinking deeper the longer you stay. Tap a line to play it out.

        Stepping through

        • Use the arrows under the board, the ← and → keys, or tap any move or any point on the graph. Press F to flip the board.
        • Try your own ideas: move a piece on the board and the engine follows along. Back to the game returns you to what was played.

        Stockfish here is the lite WebAssembly build, a little weaker than the full engine but still far beyond any human. A full review takes a minute or so; choose how deep it looks in Settings.
